# Hero reference prompts

The three reference images were generated with the built-in image-generation tool on 2026-08-25. They establish direction only; deterministic Canvas-authored PNG fragments are the executable pack.

## Everyday layered

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: game character art-direction reference for a modular character creator
Primary request: one polished “Everyday Layered” hero character that establishes a cohesive starter-pack art style
Scene/backdrop: simple warm off-white studio backdrop with a faint grounding shadow
Subject: approachable young adult adventurer, standard proportions, long wavy indigo hair visibly passing behind the shoulders and in front as bangs, cyan fitted shirt, deep violet trousers, short plum jacket with readable rear tails, round glasses, holding a small sketchbook
Style/medium: premium 2D indie RPG character illustration; clean dark-violet linework, flat shapes with one soft cel-shaded shadow and restrained highlights; charming expressive proportions, not pixel art
Composition/framing: full body, neutral three-quarter-front hero pose, entire silhouette and shoes visible, centered with generous padding
Color palette: indigo, cyan, violet, warm rose-brown skin, small gold accents
Constraints: clearly separable modular clothing and hair shapes; anatomically coherent seams; no text, no logos, no watermark, no UI, no extra characters, no cropped limbs
Avoid: photorealism, anime fan-art styling, 3D render, excessive detail, painterly background
```
