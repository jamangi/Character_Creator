# TASK-007-HIGH-starter-asset-pack

- **Status:** BLOCKED
- **Outcome:** Ship a cohesive first asset pack that proves aesthetic range and stresses every important contract feature.
- **Depends on:** 004, 006
- **Unblocks:** 009
- **Owned paths:** `packages/starter-pack/`, art-source workflow docs, approved visual baselines, `site/validation/task-007/`, `site/validation/index.json`

## Scope

- Produce the content enumerated in `shared/STARTER_ASSET_PACK.md` under one art direction.
- Establish source templates, naming, palette-role masks, export settings, and review ownership.
- Complete and polish the three hero recipes before scaling the matrix.
- Run technical validation continuously rather than at final delivery.
- Generate and review the full combination test matrix.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] All promised assets and advertised selectors are present.
- [ ] The pack passes required validator levels and license/provenance checks.
- [ ] Three hero recipes pass expression, turnaround, and animation visual review.
- [ ] Pairwise/adversarial sheets have no untriaged seam, halo, occlusion, or ground-contact defects.
- [ ] Body-profile fit support is honest and machine-readable.
- [ ] Pack size and decode/render performance fit documented budgets.
- [ ] Pages publishes the three hero sheets and navigable combination/stress-test galleries.

## Validation

Validator report, visual sign-off checklist, regression baselines, pairwise coverage report, and measured pack/render budgets.

## Human validation

- **Required:** Yes
- **Pages path:** `site/validation/task-007/`

Publish the three hero recipes first, then the expression, turnaround, animation, pairwise, palette-extreme, and adversarial galleries. Ask the reviewer to judge aesthetic appeal, art-direction consistency, seams, occlusion, recolor quality, silhouette range, and whether the pack demonstrates meaningful flexibility.

## Non-goals

Maximizing catalog count, mixing unrelated art styles, or accepting assets solely because they are schema-valid.
